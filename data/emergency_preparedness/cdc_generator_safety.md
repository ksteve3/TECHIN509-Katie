# Carbon Monoxide (CO) Poisoning Basics

Publisher: CDC
Source: https://www.cdc.gov/carbon-monoxide/about/index.html
Captured: 2026-11-19
Format: Attributed summary

## Portable generator placement

Run portable generators outdoors more than 20 feet from windows, doors, and vents. A garage or house remains unsafe for generator operation even with its windows and doors open. Carbon monoxide from fuel-burning equipment is odorless and colorless and can cause sudden illness or death.

## Carbon monoxide detection

While using a generator, use a battery-powered carbon monoxide detector or one with battery backup in the home.
