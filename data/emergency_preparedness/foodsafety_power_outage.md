# Three Ways to Keep Food Safe When You Lose Power

Captured: 2026-11-19

## Closed refrigerator and freezer

Keep refrigerator and freezer doors shut as much as possible. With the door closed, a refrigerator keeps food cold for about four hours. With the door closed, a full freezer holds its temperature for about 48 hours and a half-full freezer for about 24 hours.

## Checking perishables after the outage

Discard refrigerated perishables such as meat, fish, eggs, and milk after more than four hours without power. Thawed frozen food can be refrozen if it still has ice crystals or is at 40°F (4°C) or colder. Avoid using a taste test to judge whether food is safe.
