# Guidelines for Personal Hygiene During an Emergency

Publisher: CDC
Source: https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/safety/guidelines-for-personal-hygiene-during-an-emergency.html
Captured: 2026-11-19
Format: Attributed summary

## Protect wounds

Wash open wounds well with soap and clean water. Cover a clean open wound with a waterproof bandage to reduce the chance of infection. Keep open cuts and scrapes away from floodwater, salt water, and brackish water.

## Warning signs

Seek immediate medical care if a wound develops redness, swelling, or drainage.
