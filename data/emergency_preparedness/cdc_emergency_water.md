# How to Make Water Safe in an Emergency

Publisher: CDC
Source: https://www.cdc.gov/water-emergency/about/
Captured: 2026-11-19
Format: Attributed summary

## Boiling water

When safe bottled water is unavailable, boiling can kill germs in drinking water. For cloudy water, filter it through a clean cloth or coffee filter, or let the sediment settle and draw off the clear water. Boil clear water for one minute at a rolling boil, or three minutes above 6,500 feet. Let the water cool and store it in clean, sanitized containers with tight covers.

## Fuel or chemical contamination

Boiling and disinfection cannot make water contaminated with fuel, toxic chemicals, or radioactive material safe. Use bottled water or another safe source and contact the local health department for guidance.
