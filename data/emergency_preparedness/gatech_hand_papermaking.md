# Early Papermaking

Publisher: Robert C. Williams Museum of Papermaking, Georgia Tech
Source: https://paper.gatech.edu/early-papermaking
Captured: 2026-11-19
Format: Attributed summary

## Preparing pulp and forming a sheet

In the historical process described by the museum, washed cloth rags were broken down into pulp. A papermaker dipped a screened mold into a vat of pulp and lifted it level. The screen held the fibers while water drained, forming a wet sheet.

## Pressing and drying

Wet sheets were transferred to felts and stacked for pressing. A wooden screw press squeezed water from the stack. The pressed sheets could then be removed from the felts and hung to dry.
