## Power outage lighting

Use flashlights for lighting during a power outage. FEMA's outage guidance calls for flashlights in place of candles.

## Emergency supply kit

Pack a flashlight and extra batteries in the emergency supply kit. FEMA lists enough flashlights and spare batteries for each person.
