## Photovoltaic cells and arrays

Photovoltaic materials convert sunlight into electricity. A cell is the individual device. Connected cells form a module or panel, and several modules can be connected to form an array. Protective layers such as glass or plastic help cells withstand outdoor exposure.

## Photovoltaic system components

Solar modules produce direct-current (DC) electricity. A system uses power conversion equipment to turn DC into alternating current (AC) used by household appliances. Mounting structures orient panels toward the sun.
